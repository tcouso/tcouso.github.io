About

I am a Computer Science Engineer from Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile working on machine learning, with a focus on reinforcement learning problems. I am currently a Research Engineer at CENIA, within its Reinforcement Learning and Robotics team. My trajectory began in neuroscience and psychology, influenced by Francisco Varela’s framework of embodied cognition. Transitioning to engineering allowed me to tackle these same systemic questions from a quantitative, computational perspective.

After finishing my degree, I set out to gain engineering experience with large-scale foundation models, and joined the team behind LatamGPT: a model trained to reflect the languages, expressions and contexts of Latin America rather than inherit the biases of imported systems, developed through more than 50 technical alliances across 15 countries. It launched in February 2026 at a public ceremony led by the President of Chile. Building it gave me hands-on engineering experience with large-scale data pipelines and distributed training regimes.

Currently, my focus is on latent world models and trajectory modelling for reinforcement learning problems. Our recent work at CENIA involves diffusion-based policies for behavioral cloning and JEPA architectures with action encoding for Model Predictive Control (MPC), where learned action representations make planning stable over long horizons.
