Research Interests

I came to AI from cognitive science and take an embodied view of intelligence. My current focus is on latent world models and planning, especially for motor control. I am also interested in exploration within planning, where the agent chooses actions to increase the world model's knowledge of the environment. My research experience covers planner-aligned action spaces for JEPA world models, deep RL for UAV control, and remote sensing for environmental monitoring. In the future, I'd like to scale this into agents that keep learning in open, changing environments, such as field robots for ecosystem monitoring and restoration.
