Research Interests

I study sequential decision-making with learned world models, especially for motor control. My central question is what an action is in latent space: how it relates to the body executing it and to the perceptual change it causes. My current work asks how to make learned world models and planners fit each other, so that what the model learns is what the planner needs at decision time.

I bring experience in deep RL for UAV control, remote sensing for environmental monitoring, and large-scale LLM pretraining with LatamGPT, where I learned how data distribution and infrastructure shape models at scale. Longer term, I want to scale world models into agents that act in open, changing environments, such as field robots for ecosystem monitoring and restoration. I first came to AI from psychology and take an embodied view of intelligence: cognition is shaped by the body that realizes it.
