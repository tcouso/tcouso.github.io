Research Interests

I'm interested in sequential decision-making under partial observability (MDPs and POMDPs), especially motor control, where policies must be sample-efficient and adapt to non-stationary environments. I approach these problems through self-supervised world models, imitation learning, optimal control, and deep reinforcement learning. I take an embodied view of intelligence — cognition is shaped by the body that realizes it — and I anticipate that breakthroughs in motor control will translate into conceptual domains.
